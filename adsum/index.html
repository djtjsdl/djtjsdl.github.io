Adsum (English)

Adsum is an Android app that shows your own estimated AdMob and AdSense earnings in one dashboard and home screen widgets. Sign in with your Google account and approve read-only report access; reports are fetched directly from official Google APIs and stored only on your device. Adsum has no server of its own.
